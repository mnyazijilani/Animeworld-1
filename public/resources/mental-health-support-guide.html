Mental Health Support Guide

A simple resource for starting safer, kinder support conversations.

Signs someone may need help

  • Major changes in mood, sleep, energy, or concentration.
  • Pulling away from class, friendships, or usual routines.
  • Talking about hopelessness, panic, or not coping well.

How to respond

Listen calmly, avoid judgment, ask simple direct questions, and help the person connect with a trusted adult, counselor, clinic, or emergency service when needed.

Urgent situations

If someone may harm themselves or someone else, urgent in-person human help should come first. Contact emergency services or crisis support immediately.
