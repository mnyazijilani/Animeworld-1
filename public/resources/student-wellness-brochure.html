Student Wellness Brochure

A short printable guide for students, schools, and families.

Daily wellness basics

  • Drink water steadily throughout the day.
  • Protect sleep with a calmer wind-down routine.
  • Use small movement breaks to interrupt long sitting periods.
  • Write down feelings before they pile up.

When stress builds

Slow the next hour down. Breathe, drink water, reduce the task list, and talk to one safe person instead of carrying everything alone.

When to reach for support

Ask for help early if worry, sadness, panic, hopelessness, or exhaustion keep interfering with school, relationships, sleep, or safety.
